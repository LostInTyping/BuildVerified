## Overview

Offender Risk Assessment and Case Planning is a multi-application umbrella serving criminal justice agencies across 20+ states for both youth and adult populations. The suite includes:

- **Risk Assessment** - Determines the likelihood of repeat offenses, helping agencies learn more about individuals and address risk and need factors specific to each person
- **Case Management** - Supports intervention and treatment strategy decisions across youth and adult populations
- **Recertify** - A companion tool for recertification of practitioners who use the case management system
